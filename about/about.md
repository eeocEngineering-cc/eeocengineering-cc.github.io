## Who We Are

EEOC was created in 2025 by two high school Bible Bowl players. 
We saw that our church paid a large amount for buzzer systems, that they were quite fragile, and that they were generally outdated in terms of design. 
We decided we could  better. Little did we know we would spend months obsessed with the project.
Initially, we planned to simply design a system that could be constructed by someone with moderate techincal ability from easily accessible parts.
We designed a highly versatile system in the hope that other quizzing organizations could use it as well.
We demonstrated a prototype, technically our third, at Bible Bowl Summer Nationals.
So many people wanted to buy a set instead of building one that we had to reevaluate our design and optimize it for batch production - the prototype system took 10+ hours to assemble.
After Nationals, we took testing results, feedback, and open minds into a redesign.
Porter taught himself PCB (Printed Circuit Board) design and translated our messy wiring into elegant, organized circuitry.
Finally, we began selling v4 buzzers at Fall Nationals, eight months after beginning the project.
We're both aspiring engineers who enjoy solving problems and making things better.

## What We Do

We design, prototype, and build quiz buzzer systems at a fraction of tratidional costs, using modern hobbyist manufacturing technology.
